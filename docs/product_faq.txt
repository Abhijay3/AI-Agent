Acme Corp Product FAQ

Laptop ($799.99): 14-inch display, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD, up to 10 hours of battery life. Ships with a 65W USB-C charger. Compatible with the USB-C Hub for extra ports.

Wireless Mouse ($19.99): 2.4GHz wireless with a USB receiver, up to 12 months of battery life on 2 AA batteries, works with Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Mechanical Keyboard ($89.99): Hot-swappable mechanical switches, USB-C wired connection, N-key rollover, compatible with Windows and macOS (Mac-specific keycaps not included).

Monitor 27in ($249.99): 27-inch 1440p IPS panel, 75Hz refresh rate, HDMI and DisplayPort inputs, built-in height and tilt adjustment stand.

USB-C Hub ($34.99): 7-in-1 hub with HDMI, 2x USB-A, SD/microSD card reader, and 100W USB-C power delivery pass-through for charging while connected.

Troubleshooting: if a wireless product won't connect, re-seat the USB receiver in a different port and replace the batteries (Wireless Mouse) before contacting support. If the Monitor 27in shows no signal, confirm the input source selected on the monitor matches the cable in use (HDMI vs DisplayPort).
