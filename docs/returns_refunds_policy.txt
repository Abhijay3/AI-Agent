Acme Corp Returns & Refunds Policy

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as they're unused and in original packaging.
Opened software licenses and gift cards are not eligible for return.
To start a return, contact support with the order ID; we'll email a prepaid return shipping label.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of the returned item passing inspection.
If an item arrives damaged or defective, we cover return shipping and offer a replacement or full refund, no restocking fee either way.
Orders can be cancelled for a full refund any time before they ship; once shipped, they follow the standard return process instead.
