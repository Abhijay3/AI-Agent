Acme Corp Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and costs $5.99, free on orders over $75.
Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $14.99.
Orders placed before 2 PM local time ship the same business day; orders placed after that ship the next business day.
We currently ship within the country only; international shipping is not yet supported.
Once an order ships, a tracking link is emailed to the address used at checkout.
If a package shows as delivered but hasn't arrived, contact support within 7 days so we can investigate with the carrier.
