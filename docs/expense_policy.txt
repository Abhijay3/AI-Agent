Acme Corp Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees can claim reimbursement for business-related travel, meals, and software subscriptions.
All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense with an itemized receipt attached.
Meal reimbursement is capped at $40 per day during business travel.
Claims over $500 require pre-approval from a department head before the expense is incurred.
Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days of approval.
